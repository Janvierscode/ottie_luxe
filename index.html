Our story

Born in Zimbabwe.
Made for your glow.

Ottie Luxe is a young, stylish Zimbabwean beauty and accessories brand built on one simple idea: looking good, smelling good and feeling confident should not mean overspending.

We bring together wearable jewellery, irresistible fragrances and gift-worthy combinations for everyday looks, workdays, celebrations and all the little moments in between.
